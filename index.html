01 About me

A research perspective.
A community connection.

Political ideas.
Everyday experience.
Independent researcher · Delhi

I am a UGC-NET qualified Political Science postgraduate and independent researcher based in Delhi. My work spans public policy, governance, electoral administration, and community engagement. My wider research interests include India's public diplomacy, the Global South, and the relationship between domestic politics and foreign policy.

Alongside research, I work with words and stories. As Research Lead and Co-Founder of Assignment Buddyyy from 2023 to 2026, I developed experience in qualitative research, literature reviews, source verification, writing, and project coordination.

Community education and menstrual hygiene initiatives have also shaped my interests, bringing questions of access, participation, and public institutions into focus.
